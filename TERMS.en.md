## 4. Data transfer on the BitTorrent network

Downloading over BitTorrent is two-way by nature: the client does not only receive data, it may also pass it on to other peers. That is, while BitTorrent is in use the user's device may transfer parts of the files being downloaded to other participants.

The app provides separate controls for this in its settings: "Upload while downloading" and "Seed after download". Both are off by default. The user is responsible for whatever materials they pass on to other peers.

While BitTorrent is running, the user's IP address is visible to trackers, DHT nodes and other peers in the swarm. That is a property of the protocol, not a feature of Typhon. The operator takes no part in the operation of that network and has no influence over it.
